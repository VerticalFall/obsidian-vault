So value migrates to players more high-beta to architectural shifts.

Companies that rely on external foundries might be less sensitive to #3 since they're not as capex intensive. 

On the other hand, companies in sectors have a lot of floating interest like certain Neoclouds, and require further financing might be the most impacted. 

> Legacy and specialty memory is a different cycle that markets conflate with AI capex.

> Wafer/substrate procurement such as ABF, SOI, InP are low cost but large downstream destruction if not secured, so we’ll likely see acceleration here as seen with $NVDA earnings call. 

> qualification cycle players within bottlenecks remain very compelling, since first revenue in a large TAM, relative to MC is material to that company.
