## Slide 2 · 研究问题与总览（1 分钟）

**EN**
So behind this whole project is one core question: when labelled financial text is scarce, which model family should we actually trust? And I investigate this through three research questions. RQ1 is about learning curves — does the generative model really win on small data, and does the discriminative model take over once we have more data? RQ2 is label uncertainty — what happens when the labels themselves are noisy? RQ3 is class imbalance — the negative class is rare, can we recover it at all?
The theoretical foundation is the classic Ng & Jordan (2002) result: the discriminative model has lower asymptotic error, but the generative model converges faster — so there should be a crossover.

**中文**
整个项目背后就一个核心问题：**当带标签的金融文本很少时，我们到底该信哪一类模型？** 我通过三个研究问题来回答。RQ1 学习曲线——生成式模型在小数据下是不是真的占优，数据多了判别式是不是就追上反超？RQ2 标签不确定性——标签本身有噪声时结果会不会变？RQ3 类别不均衡——负类样本少，我们还能不能把它识别出来？
理论根子就是 Ng & Jordan (2002) 那个经典结论：**判别式模型渐进误差更低，但生成式模型收敛更快**，所以理论上应该存在一个交叉点。

## Slide 3 · 数据集（1 分钟）

**EN**
For the data, I use the Financial PhraseBank — about 4,846 English financial news sentences, each labelled for sentiment by 16 finance experts, with each sentence annotated up to 5–8 times. There are four nested agreement subsets: 100%, 75%, 66% and 50% agreement. The classes are quite unbalanced: neutral 59%, positive 28%, and negative only 12.5%. And that rare negative class is exactly the focus of my third research question.

**中文**
数据用的是 Financial PhraseBank：约 **4846 条**英文金融新闻句子，每条由 **16 位金融专家**打情感标签，每句标了 5 到 8 次。数据集按标注一致率分成四档：100%、75%、66%、50%。类别很不平衡：中性 59%、正面 28%、**负面只有 12.5%**。这个稀有的负类，正好是我 RQ3 关注的重点。

## Slide 4 · 实验设计（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
Let me quickly explain how the experiments are set up. The key principle is fairness: both models see the exact same features — TF-IDF word and 1–2 gram, with min_df=2 and stopwords removed. So any difference in results can only be blamed on the learning algorithm, not the features.
For RQ1 I build learning curves: training sizes from 32 up to about 3,876 sentences, over 5 seeds, stratified 80/20 split, and I report both accuracy and macro-F1.
For RQ2 I use three evidence layers — within-subset CV, a fixed clean test set, and fixed-size intervals of 600 — to isolate the effect of label quality from data volume.
For RQ3 I compare four configurations to rescue the negative class.
And all evaluation is stratified 10-fold cross-validation over 5 seeds, reported as mean ± std.

**中文**
说一下实验怎么设计的。核心原则是**公平**：两个模型用完全相同的特征——TF-IDF（词和 1-2 gram，min_df=2，去停用词）。这样结果的任何差异都只能归因于学习算法本身，不是特征的锅。
RQ1 做学习曲线：训练规模从 32 到约 3876 条，5 个随机种子，80/20 分层划分，同时报准确率和 macro-F1。
RQ2 用三层证据——子集内交叉验证、固定干净测试集、以及固定 600 条的区间——把"标签质量"的影响从"数据量"里单独剥离出来。
RQ3 对比四种配置来救负类。
所有评估都是 5 个种子的分层 10 折交叉验证，报均值 ± 标准差。

【关于实验设计，答辩最可能被追问的点】"为什么用5折又不是统一的n？" → 答：为了公平，所有模型共用同一套 pipeline；RQ2 三层设计是为了把"标得干不干净"和"样本够不够多"两个因素解耦。

## Slide 5 · RQ1 学习曲线（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
RQ1 looks at learning curves. The left figure is accuracy. There, at small sample sizes, Naive Bayes and SVM are essentially tied — that's the generative "fast start". As data grows, SVM pulls ahead. At the largest scale it gets to about 0.74 accuracy versus 0.69 for Naive Bayes.
But here's the twist: the right figure uses macro-F1 instead of accuracy — and there is no crossover at all. Naive Bayes stays well below, around 0.50, while SVM climbs to about 0.68. So the Ng–Jordan prediction holds under accuracy, but not under macro-F1. **The metric actually changes the verdict.**

RQ1 看学习曲线。**左边这张是准确率**。在小样本时，朴素贝叶斯和 SVM 基本打平——这就是生成式模型的"快启动"。数据一多，SVM 就拉开差距，最大规模时准确率约 0.74，NB 只有 0.69。

## Slide 6 · RQ2 标签不确定性（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
RQ2 asks what happens when labels get noisier, using annotator agreement as a proxy for noise. The consistent message across all three figures: as agreement loosens — from 100% down to 50% — performance falls, even though more data gets added. So label quality beats label quantity.
And there's a striking number: in the 66–75% agreement band, Naive Bayes's negative-class F1 collapses to zero — meaning it stops predicting the negative class entirely. SVM drops too, from about 0.56 to 0.33, but it never fully collapses.

**中文**
RQ2 关心标签变脏时会发生什么，用标注一致率当噪声的代理。三张图传递的是同一个信息：**一致率从 100% 掉到 50%，数据反而变多了，但性能却一路下降**。所以标签质量比标签数量更重要。
还有一个很扎眼的数字：在 66–75% 这个一致率区间，**NB 的负类 F1 直接崩到 0**——它彻底不预测负类了；SVM 从约 0.56 降到 0.33，但**没有完全崩**。

【核对】三张图分别是：Fig3 子集内CV（都下降、SVM更高且更稳）、Fig4 固定干净测试集（SVM 平稳 0.76-0.78，NB 0.60-0.63）、Fig5 固定 n=600（NB 在低一致率 0.31 持续低迷，SVM 在 50-66 区间还小幅回升到 0.44）。核心一句话：**数据越脏，SVM 越稳、NB 掉得越狠。**

## Slide 7 · RQ3 类别不均衡（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
RQ3 tackles the rare negative class — only 12.5% of the data. I compare four configurations for macro-F1 and negative-class F1. With Naive Bayes, just switching from an empirical prior to a uniform prior lifts the negative recall about threefold, from about 0.15 to 0.46. But the overall winner is SVM with balanced class weights: negative-class F1 of about 0.61 and near-perfect accuracy. The message: the negative class can be recovered, and the configuration — not just the model family — decides how well.

**中文**
RQ3 啃的是稀有的负类——只占 12.5%。我比了四种配置的 macro-F1 和负类 F1。**NB 只要把经验先验换成均匀先验，负类召回就能翻约三倍**，从 0.15 提到 0.46。但总冠军是 **SVM + balanced 类别权重**：负类 F1 约 0.61，准确率接近满分。结论：负类是可以救回来的，而且**起决定作用的不只是模型家族，还有配置**。

【核对 · 原图数值】Fig.6 柱状图：NB empirical(macro 0.52 / neg-F1 0.26)、NB uniform(0.63/0.51)、SVM unweighted(0.69/0.60)、SVM balanced(0.70/0.61)。PPT 文字写"NB uniform recall×3 (0.151→0.456)、SVM balanced neg-F1 0.612"——**这是 recall 的精确数值，图上是 F1**，两者要分开讲，别混。若被追问 recall 原始值就说"在论文 §3.6/Table 4"。

## Slide 8 · 证据链汇总（1 分钟）

**EN**
Now let me pull it together. Three independent experiment lines converge on the same picture. Under accuracy, there's the Ng–Jordan crossover — confirmed. Under macro-F1, no crossover — the prediction isn't confirmed. Under label uncertainty, the generative model collapses on the negative class, while the discriminative model degrades gracefully. Under class imbalance, a uniform prior can rescue Naive Bayes, but balanced SVM is still the best. The one-line takeaway: the discriminative model's robustness concentrates exactly where the scarce negative class lives.

**中文**
现在把证据收拢。三条独立的实验线指向同一个图景。**准确率下**：Ng-Jordan 交叉——成立。**macro-F1 下**：没有交叉——预测不被支持。**标签不确定性下**：生成式在负类上崩了，判别式却优雅退化。**类别不均衡下**：均匀先验能救 NB，但 balanced SVM 依然最好。一句话结论：**判别式模型的稳健性，恰好集中在稀有的负类所在的那些样本上。**

## Slide 9 · 为什么结论是"分裂"的（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
So why is the result so "split"? Because the two metrics ask different questions. Ng–Jordan is rooted in zero-one error — every sample counts the same. That's what accuracy measures. But macro-F1 counts every class equally, and the generative model's weakness lands exactly in the class that gets the least weight in accuracy — the rare negative class. So this "split" isn't necessarily contradicting the theory — it's more like one of its boundary conditions. The generative small-sample advantage exists when errors are counted per sample, and it disappears when the rare class is counted per class.

**中文**
为什么结果看起来"分裂"？因为**两个指标问的是不同的问题**。Ng-Jordan 植根于 0-1 误差——每个样本权重大小一样，这就是准确率衡量的东西。而 macro-F1 对每个**类别**平等计权，生成式的短板恰好落在"准确率下权重最轻"的那个类——稀有负类。所以这个"分裂"未必是打脸理论，更像是理论的**边界条件**之一：生成式的小样本优势在"按样本计数"时存在，在"按类别计数"时消失。

---

## Slide 10 · 生成式的盲区（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
Now, why is the generative model systematically weak on the minority class across all three RQs? The working mechanism is this. Naive Bayes estimates each class distribution separately. The rare class only has 12.5% of the data to estimate from, so its error is sharpest at the class level. SVM never models the class distribution — it drives the decision boundary toward the rare class, and the cost asymmetry is handled implicitly through the margin of the hinge loss. The practical implication is clear: in financial text, the negative class is usually the risk signal — and that's exactly where the generative model is blind.

**中文**
那为什么生成式模型在三个 RQ 里对少数类都系统性偏弱？机制是这样：**NB 是分别估计每个类的分布**——稀有类只有 12.5% 的数据去估，所以它的误差在类别层面上最尖锐。**SVM 根本不建模类分布**，它把决策边界推向稀有类，代价不对称由 hinge loss 的间隔隐式控制。实践含义很清楚：**金融文本里，负类往往就是风险信号——而恰恰就在那里，生成式模型是瞎的。**

---

## Slide 11 · 实操建议（1.5 分钟）

**EN**
So what should practitioners take away? Two rules. First, in risk-sensitive scenarios — like credit screening or monitoring short sellers — negative news is the signal, so use a discriminative model with class weights. It gives negative-F1 around 0.61 for only a tiny accuracy cost, and it degrades gracefully instead of collapsing. Second, in genuinely small-data scenarios, below a few hundred samples with no budget for tuning, Naive Bayes with a uniform prior is almost a free lunch: threefold negative recall and interpretable class-conditional probabilities. And the broader lesson on budget: invest in label quality first, because at a fixed sample size, agreement matters more than volume.

**中文**
那从业者该怎么办？两条规则。**第一，风控敏感场景**——比如信贷筛查、做空监控——负面新闻就是信号，那就用**判别式 + 类别权重**：负类 F1 约 0.61，准确率只损失一点点，而且优雅退化不会崩。**第二，真正的小样本场景**——几百条以下、没预算调参——**NB + 均匀先验**几乎是免费午餐：负类召回翻三倍，还自带可解释的类条件概率。至于预算往哪花：**先投资标签质量**，因为在固定样本量下，一致性比数量更重要。

---

## Slide 12 · 局限与未来（1 分钟）

**EN**
Of course there are limitations. Agreement is a proxy for label noise, not random corruption — low-agreement text might just be harder. Everything comes from a single corpus in a single language. And I only compared two linear models with fixed defaults — no tuning, no significance tests. For the future, I'd like to replicate this on more corpora and languages, add deep or LLM baselines where data is abundant, and refine the agreement proxy. The goal is to test whether this generative minority-class weakness is a general phenomenon.

**中文**
当然有局限。**一致率只是标签噪声的代理，不是随机破坏**——低一致率的文本可能只是更难。所有证据来自**单一语料、单一语言**。而且我只比了**两个线性模型 + 固定默认参数**，没有调参、没有显著性检验。未来想做得更多：多语料、多语言复现；数据充足的地方加深度模型或 LLM 基线；把一致率代理模型细化。目标是想验证这个"生成式少数类弱点"是不是一个普遍现象。

---

## Slide 13 · 结论（1 分钟）

**EN**
To wrap up. On financial text, the choice between generative and discriminative is not trivial — it depends on the data and the metric. If you're risk-sensitive, go discriminative with class weighting. If you have truly small data, Naive Bayes with a uniform prior is interpretable and nearly free. And the broadest lesson: in this setting, label quality matters more than label quantity. What really separates the two models is not which is more principled — it's which side the negative class falls on. Thank you — I'm happy to take your questions.

**中文**
收个尾。在金融文本上，生成式还是判别式**不是随便选**，它取决于数据和指标。风控敏感就选**判别式+类别权重**；真·小样本就用**NB+均匀先验**，可解释又几乎免费。最大的教训：在这个场景里，**标签质量 > 标签数量**。真正区分两个模型的关键，不是谁更"有原则"，而是**负类到底站在哪一边**。谢谢各位，欢迎提问。
