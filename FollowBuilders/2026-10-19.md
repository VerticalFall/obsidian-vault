### @levie — Aaron Levie
*ceo @box - your business lives in content. unleash it with AI*

> Here’s why agent swarms are going to be a very big deal. We can finally make compute a critical variable in a large number of important tasks in the economy which wasn’t possible before.
> 
> There’s a large amount of work in the world that is essentially only constrained by the amount of time that we put into research, data processing, discovery work, running tests, and more. All of this work is bottlenecked by our ability to plan, execute, review, and coordinate everything happening in these workflows. Which means we basically do very little of them today relative to their economic value. 
> 
> Agent swarms are the first real ability to parallelize this work in a way where - in theory - the only parameter that matters is how much compute you throw at the problem. Cyber security, life sciences or material science research, trading, QA testing, data processing, and many other fields are essentially compute constrained problems in the limit once models are good enough at these tasks.
> 
> The breakthrough is that in a world where agents can use tools, write code, process data, have strong domain depth, and can coordinate with each other, you can begin to solve arbitrarily complex problems by setting the right goals and applying enough tokens to the problem. This changes a lot of fields as well as even the competitive dynamics in these fields.
> 🇨🇳 以下是agent swarms将变得非常重要的原因。我们终于可以让compute成为经济中大量重要任务的关键变量，而这在以前是不可能的。

世界上有大量工作本质上只受我们投入研究、数据处理、发现工作、运行测试等的时间量所限制。所有这些工作都受制于我们规划、执行、审查和协调这些工作流中一切事务的能力……
