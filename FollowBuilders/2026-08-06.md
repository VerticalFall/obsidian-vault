> Contrary to popular perception, most people will not adopt a new technology just because it makes them more efficient.
> 
> Instead, they’ll adopt new technology because:
> - Someone they know who’s similar to them on some dimension adopted the technology, and got positive results (eg became cooler, richer, more popular/respected), and they wanna become like that person 
> - They feel like everyone around them has adopted the technology, and if they don’t, they’ll be left behind
> 
> Notice that it’s not entirely a rational decision. It’s more of an EMOTIONAL decision.
> 
> So if you’re trying to make someone adopt a new technology, the messaging shouldn’t be “this will make you 10x more efficient!” It should be “So-and-so, someone you can relate to, has adopted this and look how much better their life has become; wouldn’t it be nice to be like them?”
> 
> This book reminds you of that. It can be distilled in one sentence: “The diffusion of technology is a SOCIAL process”
> 🇨🇳 与普遍认知相反，大多数人并不会仅仅因为一项新技术能提升效率就去采用它。
