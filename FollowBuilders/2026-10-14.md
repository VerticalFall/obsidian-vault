> Here are some example wins that we saw to put the improvements in perspective:

> 
> * Healthcare (+25 points): an intensive care management plan where both models spotted a dangerous potassium level and a drug interaction. Only Gemini 4 Argon went back and pulled the offending drugs from the final regimen, so its plan actually matched its own diagnosis.

> * Consumer products (+33 points): a product launch evaluation deciding which production expansion to fund.
